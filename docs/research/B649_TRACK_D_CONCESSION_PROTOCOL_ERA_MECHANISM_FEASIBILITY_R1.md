# B649 Track D — Concession / Protocol Era Mechanism Feasibility R1

## Scope

- `TASK_ID`: `B649_TRACK_D_CONCESSION_PROTOCOL_ERA_MECHANISM_FEASIBILITY_R1`
- `MODE`: `READ_ONLY_DISCOVERY_RECON`
- Evidence cutoff: 2026-08-15
- Question: whether the 2024-01-01 Taiwan public-welfare-lottery concession transition brought a physical-equipment or draw-protocol change that could plausibly alter B649 draw mechanics.
- Excluded: outcome/model analysis, era classifiers, consensus replay, Cohort V2, bulk video coding, and repository/database changes.

## Outcome

`VERIFIED_MECHANISM_FOUND: NO`

The concession boundary and a new Sanlih broadcast/draw studio are verified. The issuer, operating company, disclosed computer-system vendor, and public high-level onsite draw workflow show continuity. No primary evidence was found for a 2024 draw-machine fleet/model replacement, ball-inventory/specification/rotation change, or change in mixing/loading/draw settings. The venue change alone is not an evidenced physical mechanism.

Because the detailed approved operating-procedure comparison and equipment/ball acceptance inventory were not publicly located, the bounded decision is `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`, not a claim that every physical condition was unchanged. Track B conditional replay is therefore not justified.

Interpretation note: for a `NOT_FOUND` or `UNKNOWN` candidate, `PLAUSIBLE_PHYSICAL_MECHANISM: YES` means only that such a change could matter *if it occurred*; it does not mean the change was observed.

## Evidence boundary and smallest resolving acquisition

The public record is sufficient to reject the claim that the concession date *itself*, a broadcaster change, or an operator-name change supplies a predictive mechanism. It is not sufficient to prove that every machine, ball set, calibration, or low-level setting was identical across the boundary.

One narrowly scoped primary-record request could resolve the central unknown without starting model work: request (1) the fourth- and fifth-term approved draw-operation provisions/redline, and (2) the machine/ball inventory and acceptance or relocation-handover delta covering the 2023–2024 transition. No broader data collection is warranted unless those records show a real physical/procedural change.

## Decision

- No verified physical or draw-protocol mechanism was found.
- The verified venue/studio change does not, on the evidence located, establish altered draw physics.
- Do not use `year >= 2024` or the concession boundary as a predictor.
- Do not begin `B649_TRACK_B_PROTOCOL_ERA_CONDITIONAL_REPLAY_DISCOVERY_R1`.
- Treat the concession-era line as low priority: make at most the one-shot primary-record request above; otherwise close it and return Track D to a materially different information source.
