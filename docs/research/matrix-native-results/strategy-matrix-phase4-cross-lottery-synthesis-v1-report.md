## 1. Why raw comparison is not enough

Each cell's own report already states its `D_3(20)` value and notes P638
Zone-1's is "the largest of the three sealed native cells" — but a raw
probability delta is not comparable across lotteries whose random
baselines differ by ~3x (`Q_random(M3+, k=20)`: BIG_LOTTO 0.3136,
DAILY_539 0.1828, POWER_LOTTO Zone-1 0.5459), and whose `k=20` covers a
different fraction of each lottery's own cyclic shift space (`k/n`:
BIG_LOTTO 20/49=0.408, DAILY_539 20/39=0.513, POWER_LOTTO Zone-1
20/38=0.526). This synthesis's actual content is the normalization, not a
restatement of already-published numbers.

For every lottery and preregistered `k`, the machine artifact preserves all
three required estimands explicitly:

```text
ABSOLUTE_COVERAGE_DELTA(k) = Q_geometry(k) - Q_random_expected(k)
RELATIVE_COVERAGE_LIFT(k)  = ABSOLUTE_COVERAGE_DELTA(k) / Q_random_expected(k)
COVERAGE_RATIO(k)          = Q_geometry(k) / Q_random_expected(k)
```

Here `Q_geometry` is the source cell's sealed `Q_sidon`; the synthesis changes
the comparison label, not the underlying exact value. `COVERAGE_RATIO` is
exactly `1 + RELATIVE_COVERAGE_LIFT`, so both normalized views have the same
ranking while expressing different quantities.

**Finding 1 — the B649-vs-T539 ranking flips under normalization.**
Raw `D_3(20)` ranks BIG_LOTTO (0.01329) above DAILY_539 (0.00929).
Relative lift ranks DAILY_539 (5.08%) above BIG_LOTTO (4.24%) — the two
lotteries swap places once each delta is read against its own random
baseline instead of on a shared absolute scale.
(`ranking_flips_under_normalization: true` in the result artifact.)

**Finding 2 — that flip is not even stable across thresholds.** Checking
the same relative-lift normalization at the secondary M4+ threshold
(`k=20`): BIG_LOTTO 0.941% vs DAILY_539 0.281% — BIG_LOTTO is now ahead
again. So "is B649's or T539's geometric advantage bigger" does not have
a threshold-stable answer within this evidence; it is a close, lens-
dependent call, not a robust finding either way.

**Finding 3 — POWER_LOTTO Zone-1 is the one ranking element that never
flips.** It ranks first under raw delta, M3+ relative lift, and M4+
relative lift alike (`10.92%` vs the next-highest `5.08%` at M3+; `2.65%`
vs the next-highest `0.94%` at M4+) — roughly double the next-largest
relative lift at every lens tried. This is the synthesis's most robust
single comparative finding.

**Finding 4 — magnitude and growth-shape rank oppositely.** POWER_LOTTO
Zone-1 has the *largest* relative lift at k=20 but the *smallest* growth
multiple from k=3 to k=20 (7.41x, vs BIG_LOTTO/DAILY_539's 18.40x/16.81x,
which are close to each other). Its advantage was already proportionally
large at low k (`k=3` relative lift 1.07% vs BIG_LOTTO's 0.29% and
DAILY_539's 0.38%) and grew comparatively less from there, while
BIG_LOTTO and DAILY_539 started smaller and grew proportionally more by
k=20. The three lotteries do not share a single growth shape, only a
single growth *sign*.

## 3. What is genuinely common (and what isn't just a sanity check)

- `D_3(1) = 0` exactly in all three. **Not informative on its own** — a
  single ticket's coverage is identical to a single random ticket's
  regardless of geometry, by construction. This is the shared sanity
  check all three cells already used to validate their own pipeline, not
  cross-lottery evidence.
- `D_3(k) > 0` for **every** tested `k ∈ {3,5,10,15,20}` in **all three**
  lotteries, **and** the same holds at the secondary `M4+` threshold
  (`m3_and_m4_signs_agree_at_k20: true`). This *is* informative: nothing
  about a verified Sidon-type base set guarantees a priori that its
  cyclic-shift portfolio must beat random coverage at every exposure
  level and every match threshold — each cell had to confirm this by
  complete enumeration, independently, in its own `(n, m)`.
- Marginal advantage increasing (not diminishing) across the tested
  ladder, in all three (`geometry_advantage_zero_crossing: NONE` in all
  three source cells) — the same qualitative shape, even though the
  three lotteries disagree on how much it grows (Finding 4 above).
- The degenerate exact-match collapse (`D_M(k) = 0` for every `k` when
  `M` equals the lottery's own `draw_size`) occurs correctly in
  DAILY_539 (`M5`) and POWER_LOTTO Zone-1 (`M6`), each independently
  re-derived and each correctly explained by the identical reasoning
  ("matching only ever means being the literal winning combination").
  This is a computational-consistency check across differently-shaped
  cells, not a mechanism finding by itself — but its correctness in both
  places is what makes the rest of the comparison trustworthy.

**Constructor invariant.** All three constructors are deterministic cyclic
Sidon-shift families with verified maximum pairwise ticket overlap `1`.
Their native base sets and resulting ticket sets are not identical: B649 uses
`CYCLIC_SIDON_SHIFT_B649_V1`, T539 uses `CYCLIC_SIDON_SHIFT_T539_V1`, and
P638 Zone-1 uses `CYCLIC_SIDON_SHIFT_P638_ZONE1_V1` with a backtracking
completion for the even modulus. The replicated invariant is deterministic
low-overlap geometry, not a shared ticket set.

## 4. Evidence-strength judgment

```text
CROSS_LOTTERY_SYNTHESIS_CLASSIFICATION: SUPPORTED_ACROSS_3_NATIVE_LOTTERY_STRUCTURES
EVIDENCE_CHARACTERIZATION: QUALITATIVELY_CONVERGENT_QUANTITATIVELY_LOTTERY_SPECIFIC_N3_EXHAUSTIVE_NONSTATISTICAL
```

Unpacked:

- **Qualitatively convergent**: sign, `k=1` boundary, and increasing-
  marginal shape agree across all three independently-derived native
  designs and across two independent match thresholds (M3+, M4+). This
  is the strongest part of the evidence.
- **Quantitatively lottery-specific**: magnitude (relative lift: 4.2% to
  10.9%) and growth shape (7.4x to 18.4x) both vary by roughly 2-2.5x
  across the three lotteries, and the ranking between two of the three
  is not even stable across thresholds (Finding 2). A single
  cross-lottery constant for "how much" the mechanism helps does not
  exist in this evidence — only "that it helps, in the same direction,
  every time tried."
- **N=3, exhaustive, not statistical**: this repository has exactly
  three native lottery structures, and all three have now been tested —
  this is the complete population, not a sample. No p-value, confidence
  interval, or generalization-to-a-fourth-structure claim is valid from
  three exhaustive, non-randomly-selected data points. "Replicated 3/3"
  describes internal consistency within a closed set, not statistical
  support for a universal law.
- **A caveat on how surprising 3/3 should be treated as**: part of what
  "replicated" here required was simply that a Sidon-type base set with
  pairwise overlap ≤1 could be *constructed* at all in each `(n, m)` —
  already expected and, for POWER_LOTTO's even modulus, explicitly
  anticipated and resolved by backtracking rather than treated as a
  surprise result. The non-trivial, not-guaranteed-in-advance part is
  narrower: that the constructed geometry actually beats matched-random
  coverage, at every tested exposure level, in every one of the three
  cases — each confirmed only by complete enumeration, not implied by
  the construction itself.

## No-rescue statement

This synthesis's normalization choices (relative lift over random baseline,
coverage ratio, `k/n` exposure fraction, marginal-rate growth multiple,
M3+/M4+ sign-convergence) were selected because they are the direct,
dimensionless answers to "why aren't raw deltas comparable" (Section 1),
not selected after computation to favor one ranking over another — all
were computed together in one script run and all are reported here,
including the one that complicates the raw ranking (Finding 1) and the
one that shows even that complication isn't stable (Finding 2).
