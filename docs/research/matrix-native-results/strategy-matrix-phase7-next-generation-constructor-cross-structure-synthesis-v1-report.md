## 4. Common invariants vs. structure-specific differences

**Common to all three:**

- Constructor E exactly **ties** the previous reference at k in {1,3,5} in
  every structure (`ΔE_vs_ref = 0` exactly, 9/9 cells) — no downside observed
  anywhere in the tested ladder.
- The first strict improvement appears at **k=10** in all three, coinciding
  exactly with `max_pairwise_overlap` first reaching `1` for both arms (the
  disjoint-block region — where zero overlap is achievable by construction —
  is exhausted by k=10 in every structure's own pool/draw geometry).
- `RELATIVE_LIFT_RANDOM(k)` is monotonic non-decreasing through k=20 in all
  three (keeps growing with more tickets, uniformly) — the same qualitative
  invariant the Phase-5 arm-B-vs-Sidon cross-lottery synthesis already found
  for its own vs-random measure.
- `max_pairwise_overlap` is **tied**, never reduced, at every k where E beats
  the reference; the entire coverage gain is delivered through
  `sum_pairwise_overlap` alone (Q6). Zero duplicate tickets everywhere.

**Structure-specific:**

- `RELATIVE_LIFT_REFERENCE(k)` is monotonic non-decreasing through k=20 in
  Structures A and B, but **not** in Structure C: Structure C peaks at
  k=15 (+3.0716%) and is lower at k=20 (+2.8775%) — the only one of the
  three still not climbing at the top of the tested ladder.
- Magnitude at k=20 varies by roughly 2x across structures and does not rank
  in the same order as runtime: `RELATIVE_LIFT_REFERENCE` at k=20 is
  Structure C (2.88%) > Structure A (2.28%) > Structure B (1.10%) — Structure
  B has the thinnest reference-relative margin of the three.
- Sealed total runtime for the full k<=20 ladder spans roughly 25x across
  structures: Structure B ~65.6s, Structure C ~338.2s, Structure A ~1609.1s
  (largest search space, largest runtime — unsurprising, not itself a
  mechanism finding).
- Schema evolution: Structure A (the discovery/advance cell) predates the
  `sum_pairwise_overlap` / `lex_objective_{b,e}` fields and the named
  `geometry_lex_max_sum_not_increased_where_coverage_superiority_claimed`
  gate clause that Structures B and C (both later replication cells) carry
  natively; Structure A alone carries `q_c_sealed`,
  `frontier_capture_ratio_e`, and `b_to_c_gap_capture` (§5), which Structures
  B/C do not and were never asked to.

## 6. On the derived `sum_pairwise_overlap` for Structure A

Structure A's sealed geometry schema records `max_pairwise_overlap`,
`overlap_one_pair_count`, and `mean_pairwise_overlap`, but not
`sum_pairwise_overlap` directly (Structures B/C's later schema added it).
When every pairwise overlap value is 0 or 1 (`max_pairwise_overlap<=1`, true
at every row in Structure A's own sealed geometry, for both Constructor E
and the reference), the sum of overlaps over all pairs equals exactly the
count of pairs with overlap 1 — `overlap_one_pair_count`. This synthesis
verifies `max_pairwise_overlap<=1` before applying the identity at every
cell (`sum_pairwise_overlap_derivation:
DERIVED_FROM_OVERLAP_ONE_PAIR_COUNT_MAX_LE_1` in the machine artifact) and
would raise rather than silently apply it if that precondition ever failed.
